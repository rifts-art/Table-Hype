## 🖤 About TABLE HYPE

**TABLE HYPE** is a modern, web-based watch-party application designed to bring people together through shared video experiences.

Whether friends are in the same room or miles apart, TABLE HYPE aims to make watching videos together simple, interactive, and accessible.

Built around synchronized playback, room-based viewing, and peer-to-peer communication, the project explores ways to make shared entertainment possible without depending entirely on expensive centralized video streaming infrastructure.

### 🎯 Our Vision

To create a modern watch-together platform that connects people through entertainment while exploring efficient, accessible streaming technologies.

## ⚡ Streaming Architecture

TABLE HYPE explores different approaches to shared video streaming.

### 1. Peer-to-Peer Streaming

Connected devices communicate directly when a suitable connection can be established.

This approach may reduce dependence on centralized media delivery.

However, latency, resolution, and connection reliability depend on network conditions and implementation.

### 2. Centralized or Proxied Streaming

A server or media service handles some or all of the video delivery.

This can be useful in certain scenarios but may introduce bandwidth, infrastructure, and operating costs.

TABLE HYPE aims to explore efficient approaches to shared streaming while balancing quality, accessibility, and cost.

**Note:** P2P does not automatically guarantee zero latency, unlimited bandwidth, or high-resolution playback.
